Privacy Policy

Exposure collects no personal data, requires no account, and keeps everything on your device.

Data summary

Rolls, cameras & settingsSaved locally inside the app's sandbox on your device (rolls.json and UserDefaults). No location is recorded.
Accounts, analytics & adsNone. No third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs are included.
Network trafficStoreKit (Apple) only, when loading prices, purchasing, or restoring Exposure Pro.

No data collection

Exposure does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, usage telemetry, crash logs, or device identifiers. Everything you enter — film stocks, camera profiles, calibration offsets, and roll logs — stays exclusively on your iPhone or iPad.

Camera permission

Camera access is requested only after you tap Start the meter. Exposure reads the camera hardware's exposure duration, lens aperture, ISO, and exposure target offset to calculate EV. Video frames are discarded immediately on arrival and are never saved to Photos or uploaded.

Deleting your data

To erase all logged rolls and frames at once, open Settings › Delete all rolls. Deleting the app removes all remaining preferences and camera profiles from your device.

In-app purchase

Exposure Pro is a single non-consumable purchase processed directly by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment details or Apple Account credentials.
