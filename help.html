Using Exposure

  1. Film and camera. Tap the top bar to set box speed and exposed ISO (push/pull), then define your cameras: fastest and slowest shutter speeds (whole, half, third stops, or older 1/25–1/50–1/100 sequences), B mode, lens aperture clicks, or a fixed-aperture pinhole. Exposure answers only with settings that camera can actually set.
  2. Spot and whole-frame metering. Point your iPhone at the subject. Tap any point in the viewfinder to spot-meter it, or tap Spot to return to whole-frame averaging. Hold reading freezes the EV with its elapsed age while you adjust your camera.
  3. Shadow, highlight & scene band. In harsh contrast, tap the deepest shadow that must keep detail, wait briefly for auto-exposure to settle, and tap Shadow. Tap the brightest area and tap Highlight. The Scene band compares both against your film's detail window (default −2 to +2 EV, adjustable in Film and camera). Place shadow, middle, or highlight, or drag the ruler in thirds of a stop.
  4. Out-of-range limits. When the light exceeds your camera's fastest speed or smallest aperture, Exposure hatches unavailable marks and offers a one-tap fix such as stopping down, slowing the shutter, or adding an ND filter.
  5. Exposure Pro (one-time purchase). Adds the Roll Log (load a roll, write frames in one tap with aperture, speed, EV, scene ends, notes, and text/CSV export) and Long Exposure tools (ND2 to 16-stop filters, HARMAN technology ILFORD/KENTMERE reciprocity Tc = Tm^p or custom exponent, and a B-shutter countdown timer).

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is an iPhone as a light meter?An iPhone camera estimates reflected light from its auto-exposure system; it is not a lab-calibrated meter. Match it once to a trusted meter in Settings › Match my meter, or dial a −3 to +3 EV calibration offset stored locally on your device.
Does Exposure take or save photos?No. The camera stream is used solely to read auto-exposure parameters (aperture, exposure duration, ISO, target offset). No frame is captured, stored, or transmitted.
Can I use Exposure without camera access?Yes. Tap By hand in the finder to dial EV directly in thirds of a stop or select a row from the Kodak TRI-X F-4017 daylight exposure table.
I already bought Exposure Pro — how do I restore it?Open Settings (top right) and tap Restore purchase using the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
